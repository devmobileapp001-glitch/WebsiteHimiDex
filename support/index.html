App and game support

Please include the product name, app version, phone model, Android version, and a description of the issue in your email. You may attach screenshots with personal information hidden to help us understand the problem.

Correction or deletion requests

Email us from the address you previously used to contact us, use the subject “Data request,” and describe the information you would like to review, correct, or delete. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

The website has no accounts to delete. For apps and games, data management depends on the product; include the app name so HimiDex can provide the right guidance. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily delete backups or data stored by external services.
