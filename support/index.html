App & game technical support

When reporting a technical problem, crash, or bug, please include as much detail as possible to help us reproduce and fix it quickly:

  • Product name (e.g. Himi Tools, CatGun) and installed app version
  • Device model and Android OS version (e.g. Samsung Galaxy S23, Android 14)
  • Clear description of what happened and steps to reproduce
  • Screenshots or error messages (with any personal data redacted)

Data correction or deletion requests

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety requirements and global privacy standards, you can request review or deletion of any data associated with your interaction:

Email us at devmobileapp001@gmail.com with the subject “Data Request” and describe what information you would like to inspect or delete. For safety, please do not send passwords, card details, or verification codes.

Good to know: Our website does not require registration or user accounts. For our Android apps and games, data management depends on the specific title. Most apps store settings locally on your device, which you can reset at any time via Android App Info > Clear Storage.
