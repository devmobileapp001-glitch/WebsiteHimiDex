Privacy Policy

HimiDex Studio  ·  Contact: devmobileapp001@gmail.com

This policy applies to the HimiDex official website and all Android applications and mobile games published by HimiDex on Google Play and related distribution channels (including the CatGun project and Himi utility apps). Our products prioritize privacy, store user data locally whenever possible, and do not require mandatory account creation or social sign-in.

Data in our Android apps & mobile games

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal diagnostic and performance data required to keep apps and games running smoothly and securely.

Where third-party ad services such as Google AdMob are utilized to support development, Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • IP addresses: used by Google to determine coarse geographical location for region-appropriate service.
  • Device & Advertising IDs: such as Android Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set ID, subject to your Android device settings.
  • In-app interactions: launch counts, error logs, and basic ad interactions to measure stability and prevent ad fraud.
  • Technical diagnostics: ANR (Application Not Responding) reports, crash stack traces, and device hardware profile.

For more technical details, review the Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure.

Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

To improve stability and ensure games and apps run without lag across thousands of diverse Android hardware configurations, we may utilize Firebase Analytics. Firebase collects aggregated, pseudonymized metrics such as session duration, feature usage, and crash reports. We do not collect personally identifying records such as names, physical addresses, or phone numbers through analytics.

You can learn more about Google's data handling through the Firebase Privacy Overview.

Advertising choices and device controls

You maintain full control over personalized ads directly from your Android operating system settings: navigate to Settings > Privacy > Ads on your Android device to reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ad targeting. You may also visit Google My Ad Center.

Local device storage & data deletion

All tool configurations, preferences, and local game states are saved directly on your local device storage. You can delete all local data at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage > Clear Data, or simply uninstalling the application.

1. Website visits

The HimiDex website does not use tracking cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party marketing tags. Standard web server logs may record basic HTTP connection data (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for system administration and security purposes.

2. Support inquiries

When you email us at devmobileapp001@gmail.com, we will only use your correspondence to assist you with your question or bug report. We never sell or rent your contact details to third parties.

3. Children's privacy

Our applications and games are compliant with Google Play Families Policies where applicable. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).

4. Policy updates & contact

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new product features or regulatory updates. For any questions or data requests, contact our privacy lead at devmobileapp001@gmail.com.
