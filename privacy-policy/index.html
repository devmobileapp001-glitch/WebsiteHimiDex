Privacy Policy

HimiDex · Contact: devmobileapp001@gmail.com

This policy applies to the HimiDex website and Android apps and games published by HimiDex that link to this policy, including the CatGun project. These products use Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics and do not require account creation or sign-in. Last updated: September 20, 2026.

Data in our apps and games

No sign-in does not mean that no data is processed. Google AdMob serves in-app advertisements and may collect and share data through the Google Mobile Ads SDK:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate an approximate location.
  • Android advertising IDs, app set IDs, and other device or account identifiers applicable to the SDK configuration.
  • Interactions with apps and advertisements, such as app launches, views, and taps.
  • Diagnostic information, including errors, performance, and technical device data.

This data supports ad delivery and measurement, performance analysis, and fraud prevention. The actual scope depends on the SDK version, device, advertising configuration, and your choices. See the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Firebase Analytics

HimiDex uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to understand how users interact with our apps and games. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect app events and user properties, such as app opens, session activity, screens or features used, device and app information, language or locale, and a Firebase installation identifier. Where enabled by the app configuration and device settings, it may also collect the Android advertising ID.

We use this information in aggregated form to measure product performance, identify which features are useful, improve stability and gameplay, and understand how our apps are used. HimiDex does not use Firebase Analytics to require account creation or to identify you by name, email address, or phone number. Learn more from Firebase's Analytics documentation and data collection controls.

Advertising and your choices

Advertisements may be personalized depending on your settings and consent where required. You can manage advertising preferences in your Android device's privacy settings and My Ad Center. The location of these settings varies by device and Android version. Turning off personalization does not remove all advertising or all processing of advertising-related data.

Information processed by Google, including information processed through AdMob and Firebase Analytics, is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and its explanation of how Google uses information from partner apps. Google may process data on servers outside your country in accordance with its policy. Google determines data retention periods based on processing purposes and applicable obligations.

Local data and deleting app data

Settings and game progress may be stored on your device, depending on the product. You can clear app data in Android settings; doing so may erase your progress. Uninstalling an app does not guarantee deletion of operating system backups or data already processed by advertising providers. HimiDex does not provide sign-in accounts that require an account deletion process.

To make a request about data held by HimiDex, email our support address with the app name and details of your request. For data managed by Google, you can also use Google's privacy tools. HimiDex cannot promise to directly delete data outside its control.

1. Website visits

The website has no registration forms, user accounts, behavioral analytics tools, or advertising code installed by HimiDex. The website content does not use cookies or browser storage to track you.

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, access times, and request logs to deliver content, maintain security, and operate the service. The provider's activities are governed by its own policies.

2. When you contact support

When you email HimiDex, you provide your email address, message content, and any attachments you choose to include. HimiDex uses this information to respond, troubleshoot issues, and handle your request. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment information.

3. Data sharing

HimiDex does not sell the contents of support requests. Email and infrastructure providers may process information to deliver their services. Information may also be disclosed when necessary to respond to a valid legal request.

4. Retention and security

Support information is retained only as long as necessary to handle your request and related obligations. The specific period depends on the case and applicable retention requirements. HimiDex restricts access to support correspondence; no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted by contacting devmobileapp001@gmail.com. HimiDex may need to verify ownership of your email address before processing a request. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

6. Children and sensitive data

The website and apps do not require user profiles. HimiDex does not actively ask children to submit personal information through support channels. For games intended for children, advertising settings and parental notices must be appropriate for the age group and release market. If you believe a child has submitted personal information by email, a parent or guardian can contact us to request review and deletion.

7. Apps, games, and external services

Products covered by this policy use AdMob and Firebase Analytics and do not require sign-in. If a product introduces other data processing, the relevant policy needs to be updated to reflect the change. External links, where present, are subject to the policies of the destination services.

8. Policy updates

HimiDex will update this page and its revision date when website practices change. Please send any privacy questions to the support email listed above.
